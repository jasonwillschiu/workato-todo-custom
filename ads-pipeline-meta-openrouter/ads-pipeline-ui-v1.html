Sign in with Google to generate ad creatives, test them against simulated Meta Ads data, and iterate on the winner. Your sessions are saved to your account only.

Sessions

1 · Round 1 brief

Describe the ad, pick a model and a format. Three images are generated in parallel.

2 · Meta Ads results

Simulated Meta Ads data, saved in the Ads Meta Metrics data table. Each click re-randomises the results.

3 · Round 2 variations

Keep the winner's hook and visual fixed, change one thing. The prompt is pre-filled and you can edit it.

4 · Round 2 results

Simulate Meta Ads data for the variants and compare against the round 1 winner.
